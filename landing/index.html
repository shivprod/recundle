How it works

Set up in under a minute

  1. Sign in with Google

    Use the Google account where your receipts arrive. No new password to remember.

  2. Sync your Gmail

    Allow read-only access. Recundle scans your receipts and invoices for recurring payments.

  3. See every renewal

    Your subscriptions, sorted into needs attention, this week and coming up, with your monthly total on top.

Your inbox stays yours

Read-only, receipts only

Recundle needs your email to find receipts. It uses that access for nothing else.

  • Read-only accessRecundle can't send, delete or change any email.
  • Only receipts and invoicesIt looks for payment emails and ignores everything else.
  • Disconnect any timeOne tap in the app removes Recundle's access to your Gmail.
